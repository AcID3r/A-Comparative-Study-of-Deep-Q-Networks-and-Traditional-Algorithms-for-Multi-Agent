## Protocol and audit findings

Frozen development config: 5×5, corner endpoints, .15 wall probability, horizon
50, 100 training/10 validation/50 test geometries, five seeds, 2,000 episodes
per seed. Train/validation/test maps exclude each other and CP-04's fixed board.
Initially unreachable counts are 4/0/1 respectively. Seven duplicate candidate
geometries were rejected; unreachable maps were not filtered out.

Training audit verified seeded map selection, rewards, epsilon/counters,
target-copy flags, split exclusion and final-model validation reloads. Evaluation
audit replayed every saved action, observation, reward, position and graph version;
verified checkpoint digests and identical realized changes on every test map.

Dynamic changes occur before observations/actions: add a distant BFS-route cell
at tick 3, remove at tick 8. Occupancy rejection is tested separately; the default
cell is too distant to be occupied before tick 3. Planners replan on changes;
frozen A* retries its original route. DQN evaluates without online learning.

## Interpretation and unfinished work

Varied-map training produced more held-out successes than fixed-map training in
this development bank, while classical methods succeeded more often. This does
not establish a general superiority claim or a statistical significance result.
Five seeds share the same 50 test maps, not 250 independent map draws. Means of
moves and arrival include only successes, which differ between methods.

Full-map classical observations differ from local DQN observations. Endpoints,
grid size and density are fixed. Dynamic tests transfer from static training.
An obstacle on BFS's path may leave A*'s path unaffected; frozen and replanning
A* tied here. The schedule does not establish adaptation superiority. Dynamic
optimality gaps are missing rather than based on an invalid static reference.
Timing samples are diagnostic, not controlled speed benchmarks.

Training-success and final-comparison PNGs were visually inspected. Tables and
curves come from saved measurements; no measured values were manually changed.

Multiple agents, occupancy features, reservations, shared-policy training,
